Mortgage questions we get every week

How much do I need for a down payment in Huntsville?

It depends entirely on the loan. VA purchase financing is typically zero down; conventional below 20% down carries mortgage insurance until you reach that threshold; FHA accepts lower credit scores with insurance that generally lasts for the life of the loan. Zero down is not automatically cheaper — it is more loan at the same rate.

Are there first-time home buyer programs in Alabama?

Yes — the state's housing finance agency runs Alabama's homeownership programmes, including down-payment assistance for qualifying buyers, and some counties add their own. We do not quote programme amounts here, because eligibility rules change and a figure we cannot verify is worse than a link.

Do I need a VA loan if I am military?

No — you can compare VA against conventional and FHA. Many buyers near Redstone find VA is cheapest on total cost because of the zero-down structure and no monthly mortgage insurance; others find conventional wins on a specific property or timeline. Ask for both side by side.

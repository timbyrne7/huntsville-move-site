The shape of the market, in five numbers

Government & defence

58,360

Five employers, and the reason the metro exists economically: the Army installation plus NASA Marshall, the FBI, the City of Huntsville and Madison County. Redstone alone is larger than every private employer on this list combined.

R&D, engineering & software

24,276

Eighteen firms — Northrop Grumman, Boeing, Leidos, SAIC, Lockheed Martin and a long tail of employee-owned and mid-size integrators (Torch, COLSA, Intuitive, MTSI, i3). This is the cluster that makes Huntsville unusual: it is not a branch office town, it is where the work is done.

Manufacturing & aerospace

17,951

Fourteen employers. Mazda Toyota and Toyota Alabama anchor the automotive corridor, Blue Origin and L3Harris the engine work, with PPG, Kohler, BASF, YKTA, ADTRAN and Sanmina supplying them. Shift patterns here drive very specific commute questions.

Health care

14,009

Two systems: Huntsville Hospital (12,508) and Crestwood Medical Center (1,501). The largest private-sector employer in the region, and a workforce that cannot be remote — so it anchors demand around the medical district.

Education

10,169

Six employers: three school systems, UAH, Alabama A&M and Calhoun. Teaching and university jobs are the reason some buyers care about a specific district boundary rather than a general area.

Logistics, finance & utilities

4,952

Amazon and Target's distribution centres, Redstone Federal Credit Union, Huntsville Utilities, Phoenix, and the Space & Rocket Center — smaller individually, but they explain a lot of the shift-based commuting on the western and northern edges.

What that means for where you live

The three commutes that decide it

  • Redstone Arsenal (Gate 9, Rideout Road). The metro's biggest single traffic generator. From Madison it measured 6.8 miles and 11.6 minutes off-peak on live road routing; at shift change, budget more. Non-DoD ID holders need a pass and a sponsor, so the first trip is a planning exercise.
  • Research Park and Cummings Research Park. The contractor cluster sits between Redstone and the city, which is why Research Park Boulevard inbound is one of the four real bottlenecks here.
  • The automotive corridor. Mazda Toyota and Toyota Alabama pull from Limestone County and west Madison — often the difference between a 20-minute and a 45-minute drive for the same house budget.

Things people get wrong about this job market

  • "It's all defence." Defence and government are 45% of the listed headcount, but health care alone is 14,009 jobs and manufacturing is 17,951.
  • "Contractor = short contract." Several of these firms have been here for decades and hold multi-year programs; others are program-funded. Ask about the contract, not the company size.
  • "Clearance or nothing." Many roles require eligibility for a clearance rather than an active one, and a large share of the hospital, school, manufacturing and logistics jobs require none.
  • "Remote means I can live anywhere." True for some software roles— but if you go into an office twice a week, the commute maths still applies twice a week.

Employer questions, answered

What is the largest employer in Huntsville?

Redstone Arsenal, at 45,500 people across the installation — Army organisations plus NASA Marshall (7,000), the FBI (2,200) and the contractor workforce that supports them. The largest single non-government employer is Huntsville Hospital at 12,508.

Are there jobs here outside defence and space?

Yes, and in volume: 14,009 health-care jobs, 17,951 in manufacturing and aerospace, 10,169 in education, plus logistics, finance and utilities. Mazda Toyota alone is 4,000 people and has been hiring continuously since it opened.

Do I need a security clearance?

For many contractor and federal roles you need to be eligible for one — which is a background process, not a credential you must already hold. A large share of roles at the hospital, schools, manufacturers and logistics sites require no clearance at all. Federal openings are posted on USAJOBS; contractors post their own.

What do people here earn?

Median household income is $86,499 in Madison County and $74,714 in Huntsville city (ACS 2024 5-year) — well above the national median. Individual pay varies enormously by sector, so we don't publish a single "average salary" figure for the city.

What that buys here

Which employer should decide where I live?

Whichever one you'll drive to at 7:40am. The single most useful thing you can do before choosing an area is measure your real route and start time — the commute calculator does it on live road routing, and the difference between two areas is often 20 minutes a day.

Is the job market still growing?

We link the Chamber's current list rather than forecast it — employer counts move every year and we would rather you read the primary source than our summary of a trend. What the list does show is a market with no single point of failure: federal, contractor, health, manufacturing and education are all large.

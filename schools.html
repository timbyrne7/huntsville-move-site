Schools in Huntsville, AL: eight systems, three counties

Madison County has three separate public school systems, and once you look west into Limestone and Morgan counties there are five more. None of the boundaries follow city limits or mailing addresses. That single fact is behind more moving regret here than price.

How to check any address in five minutes

  • Open the Huntsville City school locator and search the address. Confirm the elementary, middle and high school — all three, not just the first.
  • If the address is in Madison city, check the district's own zone page instead, because it uses the city boundary and lists the Limestone County entries separately.
  • If the address is unincorporated Madison County, use the Madison County zone page.
  • If it is in Limestone County, the district is Limestone County Schools — unless the address is inside Athens city limits, which is Athens City Schools.
  • If it is in Morgan County, it is one of three: Decatur City, Hartselle City or Morgan County Schools.
  • Then open the school on the Alabama State Report Card and read its actual academic indicators instead of a rating-site score.
  • Finally, ask the district about current capacity in that grade band. Attendance lines move when a school fills, and new subdivisions are where that happens first.

School questions we get asked

What is the best school district near Huntsville, Alabama?

There is no defensible single answer, so here is what can be verified: Madison City Schools runs 8 elementary, 3 middle and 2 high schools; Madison County Schools is the largest system by campus count on this side of the region at 20,500 students across 30 campuses; Huntsville City Schools is the largest system serving in-city neighbourhoods plus Hampton Cove, Big Cove and Green Mountain.

Compare specific schools on the Alabama State Report Card rather than a star rating.

Huntsville City Schools vs Madison City Schools — what is the difference?

They are separate systems serving different areas. Madison City covers the city limits plus the Town of Triana by agreement and part of Limestone County — a reported 27% of its students live in the Limestone County areas of Madison. Huntsville City covers the city itself plus Hampton Cove, Big Cove and Green Mountain, where it does not run a bus route over the mountain.

Sources: Madison City Schools zone page; al.com reporting; Green Mountain Civic League.

Does my mailing address tell me the district?

No, and this is the single most common costly mistake movers make here. Hazel Green, New Hope and the Ryland/Central area are Madison County Schools communities even where the mailing address says Huntsville. Unincorporated addresses are county-district addresses by default.

How do I look up my school zone?

Use the Huntsville City locator for city addresses, the Madison City zone page inside Madison, the Madison County zone page for unincorporated addresses, and the district site for Limestone, Athens City, Morgan, Decatur City and Hartselle City. Check all three grade bands before you commit.

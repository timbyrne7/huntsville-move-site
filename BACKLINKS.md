# Backlinks for movetohuntsville.net — how to

Written 2026-10-21. Companion file: `backlink-targets.csv` (109 verified URLs across
71 live domains, each fetched and confirmed HTTP 200 — no invented URLs).

---

## 1. Reality check before you spend a minute on this

Two different search surfaces, two different jobs:

| Surface | What drives it | Do links matter? |
|---|---|---|
| **Map pack / local pack** ("mortgage broker Huntsville") | Google Business Profile, reviews, proximity, NAP consistency | Barely. GBP + reviews are the whole game. |
| **Organic pages** (your cost-of-living, property-tax, PCS pages) | Content relevance, links, freshness | Yes — this is where links pay. |

So: **do the GBP work first**, then links. A site with 5 good local links and 60
reviews beats a site with 50 directory links and no reviews.

Three things that are true and worth internalising:

- **Relevance beats authority.** One link from `redstone.armymwr.com` or the Redstone
  Rocket is worth more than fifty generic directory listings, because both the topic
  and the audience match.
- **Most easy links are `nofollow`.** Chamber directories, mover listings, social
  profiles. They don't pass PageRank — they pass *discovery* and *trust*, and they get
  you found. Take them, but don't confuse them with the ones that move rankings.
- **You already own the asset that earns real links.** Free calculators built on
  original Census data (cost of living, rent vs buy, property tax, commute, PCS
  shortlist) are exactly what local journalists, realtors and base offices link to.
  Most local lenders have nothing linkable; you do. Lead with that.

## 2. Tier 1 — foundations (do these first, mostly one-off)

Citations, memberships and profiles. Individually weak, collectively they establish
that this is a real, locally-rooted business — which is what the local pack reads.

Priority order:

1. **Google Business Profile** — not a backlink, but it outranks everything here for
   local intent. Category: Mortgage broker / Loan agency. Service area: Huntsville,
   Madison, Athens, Decatur. Add every page of the site as a service.
2. **NMLS Consumer Access** — your licence record. Make sure the business name and
   website match the site exactly. This is a trust citation and a compliance item.
3. **Huntsville/Madison County Chamber** (`hsvchamber.org`) — membership buys a member
   directory listing, a news mention and the events where you meet the realtors who
   refer you. The single best paid tier-1 item for this business.
4. **Huntsville Area Association of Realtors** (`haar.realtor`) — affiliate membership.
   This is how lenders actually meet agents. Directory listing included.
5. **Mortgage Bankers Association of Alabama** (`mbaal.org`) — state trade body,
   member directory.
6. **ASmartPlace veteran resources** (`asmartplace.com`) — the Chamber's talent-
   attraction site has a veteran resources page; your PCS/VA page belongs on it.
7. **Still Serving Veterans** (`nrd.gov`) — Huntsville-based national nonprofit.
   Sponsorship or resource listing.
8. **Huntsville-Madison County Public Library LibGuides** (`guides.hmcpl.org`) —
   librarians curate resource lists and *want* suggestions. Email them the PCS and
   VA pages. Free, easy, high trust.

Everything else in tier 1 of the CSV is a directory or profile — work down the list,
15 minutes each, and keep the name/address/phone **byte-identical** everywhere.

## 3. Tier 2 — the links that actually move rankings

These are real local publishers with relocation content that already ranks. The play
in every case is the same: **don't ask for a link — offer something they need.**

| Target | Their page | Your angle |
|---|---|---|
| `cityblog.huntsvilleal.gov` | "New to Huntsville? Here's what you need to know" | Offer your cost-of-living and property-tax data as a citation |
| `hvilleblast.com` | "Ultimate Huntsville newcomers checklist" | Same — a data contribution to the checklist |
| `theredstonerocket.com` | The base newspaper | Pitch the VA/PCS loan guide to the editor; the audience *is* your page |
| `redstone.armymwr.com` | Relocation Readiness office | The canonical PCS resource page — ask to be added |
| `rocketcitymom.com` | Biggest local family resource | Sponsor, or contribute "buying your first home here" |
| `rocketcitynow.com`, `waff.com` | Local TV newsrooms | Offer rate/market commentary — newsrooms need quotes constantly |
| `huntsvillebusinessjournal.com` | Local business press | A market column, or supply quotes |
| `mattcurtisrealestate.com` | "Moving to Huntsville: Pros, Cons, Cost & Neighborhoods" | Ranks for your terms — pitch a data citation |
| `embrygrouprealestate.com` | "Engineer's Guide to Moving to Huntsville" | Pitch the commute/pay tools as a resource |
| `huntsvillehomesforyou.com` | "Your First 30 Days in Huntsville" | Cite your utilities/tax steps |
| realtor blogs (`iveyhsv.com`, `stevestinsonhuntsvillehomes.com`, `northalabamarealestate.blog`) | Market + relocation posts | Agent relationships; contribute a lender Q&A |
| civic associations (`huntsvillesouth.org`, `montesanocivicassociation.org`, `townmadisonhoa.com`, `bvcassociation.org`) | Community news | Sponsor an event, get a newsletter mention |

**Worked example, start to finish.** You want the link from the city blog's newcomers
post. You do *not* send "please link to my site."

> Subject: data for your "New to Huntsville" post
>
> Hi — I run movetohuntsville.net. I pulled the ACS 2024 numbers for the metro into a
> free cost-of-living and property-tax calculator, and I keep a commute-time tool that
> uses actual gate-to-desk drive times for Redstone rather than straight-line distance.
>
> Your newcomers post is the one people land on first. If it's useful, you're welcome
> to cite the figures or link the tools — no strings. Happy to pull any specific
> number you need for a section.

That's it. Specific, gives first, asks second, and offers to do work for them.

## 4. How to find more targets (repeatable)

Once the CSV is exhausted, this is the loop:

1. **Find the pages that already link to relocation resources.**
   Search: `moving to Huntsville guide`, `Huntsville relocation resources`,
   `new to Huntsville checklist`, `PCS Redstone Arsenal resources`. Every ranking
   page is a link prospect.
2. **Find unlinked mentions.** Search `"movetohuntsville"` and your business name
   periodically. Anyone mentioning you without a link gets a friendly note.
3. **Mine competitors.** Put a competing local lender's domain into Ahrefs' free
   backlink checker or Bing Webmaster Tools. Whoever links to them will link to you.
4. **Resource-page building.** Look for `intitle:resources Huntsville`,
   `intitle:links Huntsville moving`. Ask to be added to the page.
5. **Broken-link building.** Find dead links on local relocation pages and offer your
   equivalent page as the replacement. Works, and it's genuinely helpful.

### Outreach rules that decide whether it works

- Short. Under 120 words. Nobody reads paragraph four.
- Lead with what you give: data, a quote, a free tool, a correction.
- One follow-up after 7 days, then stop. Never a third.
- Send from `hello@movetohuntsville.net` — the domain now has SPF and DKIM, so it
  lands in the inbox instead of spam.
- Pitch the *page*, not the homepage. "Your newcomers post could cite this calculator"
  beats "check out my website."

## 5. Anchor text

Keep it boring and natural:

- **~70% brand or URL**: `movetohuntsville.net`, `Move to Huntsville`
- **~20% natural/topical**: `a free cost-of-living calculator for Huntsville`
- **~10% exact-match**: `Huntsville mortgage` — at most

Over-optimised exact-match anchors are the single most common way a new site gets a
manual action. You are a licensed mortgage originator; a spam penalty on the domain is
a business problem, not an SEO one.

---

## 6. What not to do

- **No paid link networks, PBNs or "50 links for $99" gigs.** These are what the link
  spam updates exist to catch.
- **No comment spam or forum signature links.**
- **No mass guest posting** on irrelevant sites — a link from a generic "business
  tips" blog with 40 outbound links is worthless and looks paid.
- **No reciprocal "link exchange" pages.** Devalues both sides.
- **No buying a mover-directory listing for the link.** Buy it only if it sends
  actual enquiries.

## 7. Tracking (10 minutes a month)

1. **Google Search Console → Links** — your authoritative list of what Google has
   actually counted. Watch *Top linking sites* and *Top linking text*.
2. **Referral traffic** — the point of links is people. Which pages send real visits?
   Those are the relationships worth deepening.
3. **Rank tracking** — track 10–15 terms ("moving to Huntsville", "Huntsville
   property tax rate", "Redstone Arsenal PCS") and watch for movement after each
   link lands.
4. **A simple log** — date, target, outcome, next action. Relationships, not links:
   a realtor who links once will link again.

## 9. Two things this site still needs first

Neither is a backlink, both block the plan:

1. **The real NMLS ID and licence state in the footer.** `assets/chrome.js` currently
   renders `NMLS #000000 (placeholder)` and `Licensed in Alabama (placeholder)` to
   every visitor. NMLS disclosure is required on mortgage advertising, and a
   placeholder is worse than nothing when you're asking realtors and the base
   newspaper to vouch for you. Replace with the real number before outreach starts.
2. **A privacy policy page.** Every form on the site promises you don't sell data;
   there's no page stating it. Realtors and newsrooms check for one, and TCPA consent
   language needs a home. It's also the page your tier-1 listings link to.

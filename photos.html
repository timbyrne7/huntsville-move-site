How the licences work here

  • Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) — usable commercially, credit the author and link the licence.
  • Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) — same, and any adaptation of the image must carry the same licence. Using them unmodified on a page is fine with credit.
  • Public domain — no conditions, but the source is still listed for traceability.
  • None of the images are AI-generated, and none are copied from a listing site or an agent's marketing material.

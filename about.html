How this site works

Short version: the numbers are cited, the school zones link to the official locators, the live BAH rates are never quoted from memory, and the mortgage relationship is disclosed in plain language.

Where the data comes from

WhatSource
Rent, home value, household income, property tax paid (57 metros)US Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2024 1-year estimates (tables B25064, B25077, B19013, B25103), retrieved via the Census Reporter API
Commute distance and driving timeLive routing on the OpenStreetMap road network (OSRM). No traffic model — peak-hour figures are labelled as an estimate
School district coverage, enrollment (where shown)Each district's own website; performance questions link to the Alabama State Department of Education report card
School zone boundaries for a specific addressOfficial district and city GIS locators — we link to them rather than redraw them
Utilities, tag and title, licensing, voter registration, pet licensingThe city, county, state agency or utility that owns the process, linked per step
BAHNot published here. Use the official DoD rate table, linked from the rent-vs-buy tool

What we deliberately do not publish

  • School ratings or "best school" rankings. District-reported data and the state report card, or nothing.
  • Live mortgage rates presented as offers. Example rates are labelled as examples until a licensed quote exists.
  • BAH figures. They change annually and by grade; a stale number is worse than none.
  • MLS listing data. Displaying it requires broker-authorised IDX access. Instead we hand you to ValleyMLS directly and use the value we can add: alerts, payment math and context.

How this site makes money

One way: it is operated by a mortgage professional in Huntsville, and people who use the tools here can ask to be pre-qualified. That's the entire model. We are not paid by agents, insurers, builders or the MLS for placement, and we do not sell contact details.

Disclosures

Every figure on this site is an estimate built from public data. It is not a quote, an offer, or a commitment to lend, and payment and tax figures will differ from your actual numbers — confirm them with your lender and with the county. We do not sell or share your contact details.

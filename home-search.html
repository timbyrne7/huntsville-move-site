Why a new window and not an embedded search? A genuine MLS search feed requires the MLS to authorise a broker's IDX display, and for a lender-run site that authorisation isn't ours to hold. Rather than show a stale or partial feed, this site hands you to the source and does the work it can do properly: alerts, payment math, and the neighborhood context around each listing.

Homes-for-sale questions

What is the average home price in Huntsville, Alabama?

The metro median home value is $331,800 (ACS 2024 1-year). That is a market midpoint for the whole metro — the price band that decides what you actually see is set by area, school district and build year, which is why the alerts below ask for the area rather than a single number.

How do I search homes for sale in Huntsville?

Use ValleyMLS — the local MLS, which is where the listings live — through the button above, and set price alerts here for the areas you care about. Set the alerts first: the alerts are what tell you a house exists in your band before the portals have indexed it.

Is the Huntsville market still competitive?

We do not publish market forecasts or call the market hot or cold. What you can verify yourself on any ValleyMLS listing is days on market, the price history and whether the price has been cut — those three tell you more about your specific band than a city-wide headline.

Should I get pre-qualified before I look?

Yes. Sellers here expect an offer to be attached to a lender letter, and knowing your payment band stops you looking at houses that will not work. Pre-qualification is a conversation about numbers, not an application, and it does not affect your credit.
